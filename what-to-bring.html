What to bring

Come prepared, leave finished.

The same list that goes out in your confirmation email. Bring what applies to you and we can usually finish in one visit.

Individual tax return

  • Photo ID for you, and your spouse if filing jointly
  • Last year’s tax return, if you are new to us
  • W-2s from every employer
  • All 1099s: interest, dividends, brokerage, retirement, Social Security, contract work, unemployment
  • 1098 mortgage interest statement and property tax bills
  • 1098-T tuition statements and childcare provider name, address, and tax ID
  • Form 1095-A if you had Marketplace health insurance
  • Records of estimated tax payments you made
  • Any letters from the IRS, the State of Ohio, or your city
  • A voided check for direct deposit of your refund

Business clients

  • Last year’s business return
  • Bank and credit card statements for the year
  • Payroll reports and copies of W-2s and 1099s you issued
  • A list of equipment or vehicles bought or sold
  • Loan statements showing interest paid
  • Your bookkeeping file, if you keep one

IRS or state notice

  • The letter itself, every page
  • The return the letter refers to
  • Anything you have already sent in reply

A note on security

Please don't email Social Security numbers, account numbers, or tax documents. Bring them with you, or call and we'll arrange a secure way to send them.

Not sure what applies?

Bring everything with a tax form number on it. We would rather hand a paper back than have to ask you to come back.
