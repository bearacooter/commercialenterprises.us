Services

What we handle.

For individuals, families, and small-to-medium businesses across Northeast Ohio. Two kinds of work: keeping you compliant, and keeping you informed.

No.Service
01

Tax preparation

Personal, business, estate, trust, and non-profit returns, prepared by the same person year after year. We file federal, Ohio, and city returns (CCA and RITA municipalities), handle extensions and amended returns, and review prior-year returns prepared elsewhere.

02

Payroll compliance

Quarterly and annual federal and Ohio payroll filings, W-2s and 1099s, city withholding, and the state-specific filings that trip up small employers: Ohio Department of Job and Family Services unemployment reports and Bureau of Workers’ Compensation true-ups.

03

Bookkeeping

Full-service books for small and medium companies: monthly reconciliation, categorized transactions, and financial statements you can hand to a lender. We work from your bank and card statements, so you spend your evenings running the business, not entering receipts.

04

Forensic accounting

When records are incomplete or the numbers don’t add up, we reconstruct and review them: for a partnership dispute, an estate, a divorce, or a business you are thinking of buying.

05

Payroll

Running payroll for your employees and managing the taxes that come with it: direct deposit, pay stubs, withholding deposits, and new-hire reporting.

06

Planning

Reporting and advice to help your business grow: entity selection, quarterly estimates, what a hire or a piece of equipment will do to your tax bill, and when it makes sense to change how you are organized.

Not sure which one you need? Book a 15-minute phone call and we'll point you to the right appointment.
