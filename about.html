Our family

Three generations on Fleet Avenue.

Founded by Henry Domzalski Sr. in 1952, carried forward by his three sons, and run today by his grandson. Same street. Same phone number.

Henry Domzalski Sr. opened Commercial Enterprises in 1952 at 5500 Fleet Avenue, a few blocks from where the office stands today. He kept the books for the shops and taverns along Fleet and did the returns for the families who worked at the mills, and he did it at a desk with a telephone, a pen, and a shelf of tax code behind him.

His three sons, Henry Jr., Thomas, and Jeffrey, joined him and carried the firm through four decades of tax law, the move up the street to 5720 Fleet in 1987, and the arrival of computers in an office that had run on ledger paper. Today Jeffrey's son Benjamin is the third generation at the desk.

The street has changed around us. The name on the awning, the phone number, and the way we treat the person across the desk have not.
